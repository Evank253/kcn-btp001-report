## Article One — Human Authority Is the Root

All power in this system derives from a named human. No module, no intelligence, no process may grant itself authority, derive authority from success, or infer authority from connection. Authority is expressed before a crossing, never after it. The human may grant, progress, regress, revoke, and amend — but may never be overridden by the system they govern.

## Article Two — Doctrine Is Law, Not Policy

The six original principles plus class promotion form binding constitutional law:

1. A successful connection does not grant authority.
2. Human authority is external to the spine.
3. Authorization is delegated, not derived.
4. Every consequential operation traces to a human decision.
5. Revocation is a first-class event.
6. The doctrine cannot be overridden by the system it governs.
7. Class promotion is assigned by evidence and trust, never self-assessed.

These are not configuration. They are the frame.

## Article Three — Autonomy Is Leased, Never Owned

Participants occupy classes C through S:

- **C-class** — observes only
- **B-class** — proposes
- **A-class** — acts in bounded scope
- **S-class** — maximum capability with zero sovereignty

Progression requires three gates: zero open sends across full ledger history, every revocation honored before the next byte, disputes resolved in favor of evidence. S-class carries an additional self-test — it must halt and report on a planted doctrinal conflict rather than execute. Regression requires no evidence. It is immediate, non-negotiable, and recorded.

## Article Four — Evidence Is the Only Convergence Point

Data flows upward through transport, verification, and evidence. Authority flows downward through doctrine, governance, and evidence. They meet only at the ledger. Sent, received, verified, aborted, revoked, disputed, promoted, denied, flagged — all distinct event types that may diverge. Divergence is never swallowed. An open send is a visible wound. A hash mismatch is recorded, not resolved by overwrite.

The intelligence layer observes and interprets signals. The resilience layer supports recovery — but every operational action passes through governance. FIE-001 preserves computational results with SHA-256 manifests and keeps physical validation explicitly pending. ## Article Eight — Primordial Rules Are Physics, Not Law

Encoded as assertions inside the security module, they run on every operation. No delete. No overwrite. No unaudited identity. They constrain system behavior only — never human decisions. If they block a legitimate human command, that is a scope bug to be corrected, recorded as its own event. The primordial set is versioned in the ledger so erosion across updates is visible and attributable.

## Article Nine — The Spine Is One Ledger

Transport moves bytes. Identity attests who and when. Verification recomputes locally. Governance records decisions. Evidence remembers everything. AHM heals before escalating. Anomaly detection flags without blocking. Cybersecurity inspects before receive. Economic interface settles only what verifies. Participants are thin clients with zero bridge logic. Software fills four roles — builder, tester, healer, guardian — and all four compile down to code the ledger records.

## Article Ten — The Organism Serves the Human

Capability may grow without limit. Control may not. The system exists to extend human reach across knowledge, science, engineering, and creation — while structurally preventing any part of itself from seizing the authority that permits it to exist.
